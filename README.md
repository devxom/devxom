# Ilia (ee-LEE-ah)

**Accessibility Architect & Consultant**

Accessibility Architect with 15+ years in technology, first in systems
administration and technical operations, then in software engineering.
I have worked with accessibility since 2017 and exclusively in that role
since 2023, integrating it into the architecture of digital products in
regulated industries. With an independent game team I am developing a
narrative 2D game built around accessibility.

## Professional practice

I have worked in international financial services since 2021. Earlier
I worked in digital publishing, including a WYSIWYG book editor, and in
global e-commerce.

My work covers accessibility architecture, design system evaluation,
audits, technical assessments and conformity documentation against
WCAG 2.2, EN 301 549, the European Accessibility Act and Section 508.

Some client and product names are under NDA.

## Independent game development

I have developed games independently since 2008. With a small
independent team based in Portugal I am building a narrative 2D game
with accessibility as its central theme. The project is in development
and has not been announced. It also serves as a platform for
investigating accessible game systems and alternative input methods.

## Technology

I write HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, GDScript, C++,
and I work on Windows, Linux, macOS, web, iOS and Android.

On the accessibility side I work with WAI-ARIA, AT-SPI, UI Automation
and NSAccessibility. I test with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver on macOS and
iOS, TalkBack and Orca, as well as forced colours, reduced motion and
keyboard-only operation.

For evaluation I combine manual audits following WCAG-EM with axe-core
and Playwright in CI, Accessibility Insights, the Xcode Accessibility
Inspector and the Colour Contrast Analyser. I report conformance as
VPAT/ACR documents and EAA statements.
